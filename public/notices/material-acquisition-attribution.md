Changes: deduplicated normal/Alpha rows into all 29 Pals from the rendered drop table; normalized drop quantity and chance; summarized the Duneshelter merchant and Ranch alternatives; and added Duneshelter coordinates from its pinned page. Because the wiki's Shops and Drops tables are transcluded, their reviewed expanded rows are retained in `material-acquisition-source.json` as an auditable source-fact fixture. The generator derives output from that fixture and separately verifies every directly stated fact plus each pinned source timestamp and SHA-256. Filtered habitat-map links go to Pindrop; no Pindrop data, map images, or game artwork are copied.

Source facts can change independently of a game patch. The guide is community reference data, not in-game verification or a claim of current-patch compatibility.
